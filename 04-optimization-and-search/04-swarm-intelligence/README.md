Swarm Intelligence
Core Concept

Swarm Intelligence draws inspiration from the collective behavior of organisms in nature.

Core Concept:

An individual agent is not intelligent on its own, but the interaction of a large number of simple agents can give rise to intelligent behavior.

Examples from Nature:

Birds flying in a flock
Fish moving in a school
Ants finding a path to food
Bees searching for resources

Differences from Evolutionary Algorithms

In GA / DE:

We have:

Population

↓

Generate new individuals

↓

Selection

Meaning:

Individuals of the new generation replace the previous generation.

However, in Swarm Intelligence:

Agents typically:

Possess memory.
Use their own experience.
Learn from the experience of others.

For example:

A bird says:

"This is the best location I found myself."

And also:

"This is the best location the entire group has found."
